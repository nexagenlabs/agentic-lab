# Methods

## Cell culture

GBM-4471 cells were cultured in DMEM containing 10 % foetal bovine serum. Low
passage cultures were used throughout.

## Treatment

Cells were seeded at an appropriate density in 96-well plates and treated the
following day. DMSO was kept below 0.5 % in all conditions.

## Readout

A resazurin reduction assay was read 48 h after treatment.
