# builds/03-triage-agent/criteria/repurposing_v3.yaml
version: 3
question: >
  Does this record report a measured cytotoxicity or viability endpoint
  for an approved drug in a liver-derived human cell model?

include_if_all:
  - id: numeric_endpoint
    text: Reports a numerical IC50, EC50 or percentage viability.
  - id: liver_model
    text: >
      Uses HepG2, Huh7, HepaRG, or primary human hepatocytes.
      Immortalised lines and primary cells both qualify.
      Transfected non-liver lines such as HEK293 do NOT qualify.

exclude_if_any:
  - id: review
    text: Review, editorial, commentary or conference abstract.
  - id: no_drug
    text: >
      No small-molecule drug treatment. A drug here means a defined
      chemical entity given at a stated concentration. Approved
      therapeutics and tool compounds both qualify.
      Crude or unfractionated extracts do NOT qualify. A standardised
      extract qualifies only if its active constituent is named and
      quantified.

on_ambiguity: flag      # never guess
