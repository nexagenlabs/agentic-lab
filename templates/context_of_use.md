# Context of Use

**Template for Chapter 11's Validation Gate.** Copy this file, fill it in for
one agent, and commit it beside that agent's criteria file and design file so
it is versioned with everything else.

One page. If you cannot complete it, the finding is not that the paperwork is
difficult. It is that the project is underspecified, and knowing that before
you build is worth more than the page itself.

Two things to hold while writing. A vague context of use does not reduce your
obligations, it maximises them, because an assessor must read it at its
broadest. And narrowing it honestly is the highest-leverage action available
here: it costs nothing, needs no engineering, and it is simply an accurate
description of a system that was always narrower than your first sentence
implied.

## 3. Model influence

*Does the output reach a decision directly, or through a person who reviews it?
If through a person: what exactly do they see, what can they change, and what
happens if they approve without reading?*

> 

*Influence is the axis you can change. Requiring human review after the output
generally reduces it, which is why an approval gate is a risk classification
instrument and not only a safety control.*
