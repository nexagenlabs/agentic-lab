# Methods

## Cell culture

GBM-4471 cells were maintained in DMEM supplemented with 10 % foetal bovine
serum and 2 mM glutamine at 37 degrees and 5 % carbon dioxide. Cells were used
between passage 4 and passage 18 and discarded thereafter.

## Treatment

Cells were seeded at 3000 cells per well in 96-well plates and allowed to
attach overnight before treatment. Compounds were prepared in DMSO and the
final vehicle concentration did not exceed 0.5 % in any well.

## Readout

Viability was measured 72 h after treatment using a resazurin reduction assay,
read on a plate fluorimeter at 560 nm excitation and 590 nm emission.
