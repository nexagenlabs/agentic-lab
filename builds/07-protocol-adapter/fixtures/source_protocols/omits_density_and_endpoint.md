# Methods

## Cell culture

GBM-4471 cells were grown in DMEM with 10 % foetal bovine serum. Cultures were
maintained between passage 5 and passage 15.

## Treatment

Compounds were dissolved in DMSO and diluted into medium such that the final
solvent concentration did not exceed 0.5 %. Plates were treated in triplicate.

## Readout

Viability was determined by ATP luminescence and normalised to vehicle-treated
wells on the same plate.
